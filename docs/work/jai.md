## Architecture in brief

JAI investigates how a multi-component AI system can perform useful reasoning while retaining explicit decision structure and control boundaries. Its approach brings together AI reasoning and logic-based control, with particular interest in backtracking, auditable evidence and human authority.

For actual implementation boundaries and the latest status, consult the [project documentation](https://leogalambos.github.io/JAI/).
